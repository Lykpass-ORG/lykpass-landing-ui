# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), chosen by the user. Landing page must be fully responsive and SEO-complete (metadata, Open Graph, sitemap, robots, structured data).

## Users

- **Attendees** (primary on this page): people in Nigerian / African cities looking for something to do — concerts, parties, talks, online events — who want real, bookable events that fit a budget, date and taste, without scrolling category filters.
- **Organizers** (strong second): people who run events and want to go from an idea to a published, priced, promoted event in minutes, with forecasts of what to expect.
- Administrators exist in the product (fraud and moderation triage) but are not addressed by the landing page beyond trust messaging.

## Product Purpose

Lykpass is an AI-native event discovery, ticketing and event-management platform. Attendees describe what they feel like doing in plain language ("rooftop jazz night under ₦15k this weekend") and get real events. Organizers draft, price, promote and forecast events with an AI co-pilot. Ticketing, payments, seating, transfers, offline scanning and payouts run on verified database records.

## Positioning

AI chooses, ranks, explains, drafts and flags — the database decides what is true. Lykpass never shows an AI-invented price, date, seat, refund status or ticket validity. Every fact shown is read from the database and validated. This "AI that cannot lie about your ticket" stance is the differentiator versus keyword-and-filter ticketing platforms.

## Operating Context

- Surfaces: public website, organizer dashboard, admin dashboard, iOS/Android app.
- Payments via Stripe/Paystack; currency shown in naira (₦) in examples.
- Virtual and hybrid events: ticket-gated "Join live" button, hosted on Lykluk Live (an African creator/livestream app) with a fallback stream link.
- Conversational follow-ups ("cheaper", "somewhere with parking"), "why this event" explanations, event Q&A assistant grounded in the event's own data, support assistant for orders/transfers/refunds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Attendee: conversational + semantic search, personalized feed (consent-only), event Q&A, smart reminders, support assistant, ticket transfers, Join live.
- Organizer: describe-to-draft event creation, demand/sales forecast with confidence range, attendance prediction, pricing suggestions with shown comparables, marketing copy and segment suggestions — organizer approves every publish and send.
- Trust: AI moderation triage and fraud scoring feed human review; AI never bans users, holds funds, approves refunds or publishes on its own.
- Fallback: if AI is unavailable, standard keyword/filter search still works.
- Launch state: pre-launch. Primary CTA is waitlist / early access email capture. No app-store badges until live.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lykpass. Lykpass uses its own brand; Eventbrite informs workflow only, never the look.
- User-binding visual constraint (revised 2026-11-27): Pantone Orange #FF5709 + white, plus one additional color chosen for a mature, aesthetic result (implemented as deep green-black #0E2622). Replaces the earlier purple + white brief.
- Motion: smooth scroll with scroll-driven animations (user referenced vayloe.com for feel).
- Must not look AI-generated or templated.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No user numbers, partner logos, testimonials, city lists or press. Future work must not fabricate any of these. Product demonstrations (mocked UI of real flows) and the grounding promise carry the proof.

## Product Principles

1. The database decides what is true; AI explains and suggests.
2. Plain language in, real bookable events out.
3. Humans approve anything touching money or people.
4. Nothing breaks when AI is off: ticketing always works.
5. Personalization only with consent.
